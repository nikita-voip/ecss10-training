# LAB-10 Troubleshooting

Goal:

Learn a structured troubleshooting approach.

Tasks:

- Verify subscriber registration
- Verify routing
- Verify SIP trunk status
- Analyze SIP signaling
- Identify and resolve a fault scenario

Expected Result:

Engineers can locate and resolve common service issues.

Key Topics:

- Troubleshooting
- SIP Analysis
- Routing
- Service Validation
